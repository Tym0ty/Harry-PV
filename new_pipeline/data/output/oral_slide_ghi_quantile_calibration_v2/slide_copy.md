Title: **Calibrate the GHI Quantiles**

Subtitle: **AgACI improves interval reliability before PV conversion**

Key takeaway:
Dynamic AgACI moves the 80% and 90% GHI prediction intervals toward their
nominal coverages before PV conversion; the improved reliability is obtained
with a modest increase in interval width.

Small method note:
- AgACI directly calibrates q05/q95 and q10/q90 endpoints.
- Interior quantiles are not independently AgACI-updated in this Stage-5 table artifact.
- q50 remains unchanged as the raw median.
- Use the later Q19 wording on piecewise-linear endpoint-shift propagation only when referring to the newer Q19 product.
